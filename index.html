How we work

From first call to launch, in four moves.

  1. Step 1

    Scope

    A technical call with an engineer. We map the skills, interfaces and milestones the project depends on.

  2. Step 2

    Match

    We propose specialists or a full team who have solved comparable problems, with the reasoning behind each choice.

  3. Step 3

    Embed

    The team joins your tools and cadence. Reporting, reviews and documentation follow your standards.

  4. Step 4

    Ship

    We stay through launch and adjust the team size as the project moves from design to production.
